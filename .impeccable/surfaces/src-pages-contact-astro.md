# Surface brief: src/pages/contact.astro (multnelis.org /contact)

Scope: the contact page at `/contact`. Visitor mode: Operate (the task is "reach the team, or the right project").
Audience: a developer who has read the landing page, or arrived from a repository, and wants to write to the team or raise something about one project.
Task: send an email to contact@multnelis.org (primary), or open an issue on a project's repository. Static site, no form: every route is a real address or URL. No people are listed.
Composition: the hero with the address as the first commit, then `Project questions and issues`, then one compact row per public project whose lane forks off main at its name, then the footer merge.
